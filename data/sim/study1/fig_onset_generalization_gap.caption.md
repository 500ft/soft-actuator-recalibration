Median absolute onset error of the segmented-quadratic onset estimator against pressure noise; true onset 0.70 of life, probe every 100 cycles, 200 trials per noisy point and one trial without noise. Panel (a): quadratic fatigue data, the estimator's own form (circles). Panels (b) to (d): logistic fatigue data with sharpness k = 8, 20, 50 (squares, triangles, diamonds); their errors are identical, so they are drawn in separate panels with the same axes rather than on top of each other. The estimator finds the onset only in data shaped like its own model: the quadratic error is at most 0.0075 of life, the logistic error is 0.40 of life at every noise level.
