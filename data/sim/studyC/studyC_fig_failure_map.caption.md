Each marker is one of the 10 held-out synthetic units, plotted as its transferred life-estimate RMSE against (a) the number of probes after its acceleration onset and (b) the distance of its rupture life from the training median (3,626 cycles), as the absolute difference divided by that median. The panels share the vertical axis. A filled marker meets the 0.10 life target (dashed line); a hollow marker misses it. Every unit with 3 or fewer post-onset probes misses the target. Error rises with distance from the training-median life (Pearson r = 0.96). The two candidate causes are collinear (r = −0.80); the coefficients are descriptive, with no inference.
