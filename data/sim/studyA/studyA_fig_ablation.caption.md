Simulation: 30 synthetic units with one dispersion axis switched on at a time and all others canonical. Each row is the noise-free standard deviation between units of the normalised P-V loop-area indicator at normalised life u = 0.9, drawn on a line from zero. A filled circle marks a spread above 10⁻⁹; a hollow circle marks a spread below 10⁻⁹, which is numerically zero. The degradation law drives the spread (0.0131); Mullins softening adds 0.00071. Rupture life, wall thickness, temperature, stiffness, time constant τ and leak stay below 10⁻⁹, as does the all-canonical control; plant-level dispersion adds nothing.
