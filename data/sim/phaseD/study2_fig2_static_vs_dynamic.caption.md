Old-life curvature RMSE of the static ridge (hollow bars) and of the dynamic corrector with 8 lagged inputs (hatched bars) under each supply topology. Both correctors are fitted per actuator on 0.1 to 0.5 of life and tested at 0.7 and 0.9; mean over the 6 held-out actuators of the Phase D cohort.
