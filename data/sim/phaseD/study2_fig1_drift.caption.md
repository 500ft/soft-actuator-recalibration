Curvature RMSE of a static ridge calibrated on each actuator at 0.10 of life and held fixed while the actuator ages; mean over the 6 held-out actuators of the Phase D cohort. Filled circles and solid line: shared manifold. Hollow squares and dashed line: isolated supply. The error stays at or below 0.035 1/m up to 0.7 of life, then reaches 0.456 1/m (shared) and 0.555 1/m (isolated) at 0.9, so the drift is similar under both supply topologies. Simulation only; no physical actuator was measured. Source: study2_results.json.
